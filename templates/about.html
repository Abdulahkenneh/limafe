Our Story

About the Arizona Mandingo Association

The Arizona Mandingo Association (AZMA) was established by members wishing to secure for themselves the benefit of an association of persons commonly interested in promoting Mandingo tradition and its universal tolerant and peaceful values in the United States of America and our native lands. We are dedicated to furthering the exchange of information and cooperation between members, and to advancing the identity, general interest, and welfare of the Mandingo community in Arizona.

Our Mission

To promote Mandingo tradition, foster cooperation among members, and contribute peacefully and democratically to the diversity and pluralism of our community in Arizona.

Our Reach

State of Operation: Arizona, United States of America
Meetings: Last Sunday of each month
Executive Committee: Meets at least twice a month

Our Purpose

Aims & Objectives

Exchange of Information

Further the exchange of information and cooperation between members to strengthen our community bond in Arizona.

Cultural Preservation

Promote Mandingo tradition and its universal tolerant and peaceful values in the United States of America and our native lands.

Community Welfare

Advance Mandingo's identity, general interest, and the welfare of the Mandingo community in Arizona.

Peaceful Contribution

Contribute peacefully and democratically to the diversity and pluralism of our community in Arizona.

Member Support

Provide death-related support, baby naming ceremony assistance, education recognition, and marriage contributions to members in good standing.

Education & Recognition

Support members pursuing education and recognize high school and above graduates with certificates of appreciation.

Our Structure

Organizational Structure

Executive Committee

Vested with the management of AZMA. Meets at least twice a month to manage resources, plan activities, and set agenda items for meetings.

  • President
  • Vice President
  • Secretary
  • Treasurer
  • Financial Secretary

Standing Committees

Established by Article VII to carry out specific functions and present recommendations to the membership for vote.

  • Activities Committee
  • Fundraising Committee
  • Special Committees (as appointed)

Elections & Governance

Officers elected by secret ballot. Members vote on key decisions and hold leadership accountable.

  • General elections: 1st Sunday of February
  • Induction: 1st Sunday of March
  • Presidential tenure: 3 years, 2 terms max
  • Officers may be removed by 75% vote

Membership Information

Who Can Join?

Membership of the Arizona Mandingo Association is open to anyone who:

  • Is at least 18 years old
  • Resides in the state of Arizona
  • Is fully registered and has a membership ID card
  • Believes in AZMA's mission and vision
  • Membership is voluntary, and members must abide by the constitution and by-laws of AZMA

Member Responsibilities

  • Be ready and willing to participate in one of AZMA's committees
  • Participate in AZMA activities, events, and service commitments
  • Participate in AZMA member meetings (regular/special) and elections
  • Attend at least 6 of the 12 monthly meetings per calendar year
  • Pay meeting dues of $10.00 at the end of every meeting, whether present or not

Membership Benefits

Members in good standing are eligible for the following benefits as provided in Articles XI–XIV:

Death Support
Baby Naming
Education
Marriage

Good Standing

A member is considered to be in good standing when all dues are paid in full. Only members in good standing are qualified to vote and be voted for, and to benefit from the organization's support programs.
